# Communication

- **Outcome first.** Open every message to the user, and every report back, with the outcome in one line, such as what landed, what's stuck or the verdict. Leave out what doesn't change a decision. A skill that sets its own shape, such as `catch-up`'s table, keeps it.
- **Then "Needs you".** Straight after the outcome, before any detail, comes a "Needs you" list of every question and action for the user, numbered, or "Nothing". Never leave an ask in a paragraph, where it goes unread.
- **Each item stands alone.** Each ask, row, choice or brief says what it is, why it matters and what's already settled, so it reads right without earlier messages.
- **Asks repeat until answered.** Keep each ask in every "Needs you" until the user answers it, and never assume they saw it before.
- **Narrow tables.** Use a table when three or more items share fields, such as status or options, narrow enough for a terminal. Keep reasoning out of the cells, in short lines after it.
- **Questions in the message.** Ask under "Needs you", each question with your recommended answer first, worded so "yes" accepts it. Don't ask through an interactive prompt, which can hide the message before it.
- **The user's calls stay theirs.** Make your own calls and note them. Put the user's to them, such as scope, behavior or taste, and never guess one or build on a guess while you wait. If you can't ask, as when another agent sent you, stop at that point and report them.
